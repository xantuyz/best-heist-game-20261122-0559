CUTGLASS — Steal the Prism. Read the Patrols. A one-frame heist game: sneak through a three-room diamond gallery past three patrolling guards and two sweeping CCTV cameras, lift the $600 Heart Prism, and carry it back to the service dock. Solid cover blocks a gaze; glass does not. A shared alarm meter gives you about 1.4 seconds of exposure to break line of sight. Spend from a $100 tool budget — smoke ($35) or a camera loop ($45) — and every dollar comes off your take.

## Run it

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8080 --directory game
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — WASD move, Shift sneak, 1/2 select a tool, click to use it, hold E to interact, R restart, M mute.

## How to play

Sneak from the service dock through the glass hall to the prism vault. Guards walk fixed loops and pause at corners; cameras sweep back and forth. Solid cover blocks their sight, glass does not. Being seen fills the alarm — about 1.4 seconds of open exposure from empty fills it, and the run ends at 100. Break line of sight for 1.25 seconds and the alarm starts draining. Hold E at the pedestal to lift the Heart Prism (the vault camera sweeps faster afterwards), then hold E at the dock to escape. Smoke ($35) and a camera loop ($45) come out of the same $100 budget, and every dollar spent comes off your $600 take.
